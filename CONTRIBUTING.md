# Contributing

Thank you for helping build a trustworthy, interoperable audio and synthesis symbol registry.

## Before contributing

Read the [README](README.md), [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), [decision log](docs/decision-log.md), and [versioning policy](docs/versioning.md).

Open an Issue before substantial new symbol-family work, external outreach, or artwork development.

## Evidence contributions

For every source, include:

- Direct URL or stable bibliographic citation
- Publisher, author, or organization when known
- Publication date or access date
- What the source directly shows
- Any interpretation separately labeled
- Relevant image, page, or time reference
- Copyright and reuse constraints

Do not upload or trace vendor, standards-body, or third-party artwork without permission. Link to it as evidence instead.

A UI control supports an observation of UI use. It does not alone establish a stable plain-text character.

## Symbol records

New or changed records should separate semantic identity, canonical name and aliases, ASCII identifier, plain-text fallback, accessibility speech, visual variants, evidence, confidence, and open questions.

Avoid assigning meaning from a particular font, product, orientation, color, axis, parameter value, or interaction state unless the record explicitly defines it.

## Artwork

Reference artwork must be original, neutral, and editable SVG. Do not add artwork before its semantic record and evidence exist.

## Unicode-related contributions

Do not describe an entry as a Unicode character, code point, or proposal candidate unless the decision log supports that wording.

## Review

Expect factual, adversarial review. A contribution may be returned for stronger evidence, clearer claims, counterexamples, accessibility text, licensing clarification, or narrower scope.
