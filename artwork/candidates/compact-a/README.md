Joshua authorized the original four-member draft on 2026-08-30, expanded active research to six sign-agnostic concepts, selected the smooth two-prong Fork A treatment, and on 2026-08-30 locked the six exact SVG hashes in [`artwork/study-locks/six-member-compact-a.json`](../../study-locks/six-member-compact-a.json) for blinded study-package construction. The lock does not approve the assets as `study-ready`, accept artwork, authorize public release, recruitment, incentives, study launch, publication, or external outreach.

The six SVGs link to the filter-response semantic records, including the provisionally approved shelf identifiers. They are unpublished working material and do not change any registry record, fallback, spoken label, standardization position, or interchange contract.

## Construction

The family uses a 24 by 24 square coordinate space, one open path per member, a 2.25-unit `currentColor` stroke, round endpoints and joins, no fill, and the same outer margins and two qualitative vertical levels. Increasing frequency is presented from left to right solely as the pilot convention documented in the candidate brief.

- High-pass and low-pass are vertical-polarity counterparts. Each has one attenuated region, one transition, and one passed region; their horizontal transition range is identical so the family does not imply different cutoff positions.
- Band-pass and band-stop are vertical counterparts. Each has one smooth, bounded interior region and no center marker or horizontal plateau.
- Low shelf and high shelf are horizontal counterparts. Their two affected-side prongs encode that gain sign is separate state; neither prong means boost or cut.
- Coordinates are presentation geometry only. They do not state a cutoff, slope, order, resonance, bandwidth, Q, gain, or notch depth.

The compact treatment intentionally favors endpoint separation and a large vertical excursion for small-size recognition. The shelf topology remains vulnerable to split, merge, crossover, and routing interpretations. The family should be compared against independently authored families rather than treated as a default.
