# Governance

## Purpose

This project maintains an open, evidence-led registry. It prioritizes accurate provenance, durable semantics, accessibility, and reviewable decisions over rapid expansion.

## Roles

- Project owner: sets direction and approves material scope, licensing, publication, and outreach decisions.
- Maintainers: validate records, preserve provenance, and merge work that meets repository rules.
- Contributors: submit evidence, corrections, artwork, schemas, tooling, or proposals for review.
- Reviewers: challenge claims, identify missing counterevidence, and assess interoperability and accessibility.

No contributor speaks for a standards body, vendor, or community without explicit authorization.

## Decision rules

A material decision requires a dated entry in [docs/decision-log.md](docs/decision-log.md) with:

- Decision and status
- Evidence and counterevidence
- Alternatives considered
- Owner or approver
- Revisit trigger

Material decisions include registry scope, semantic identity, canonical names, artwork acceptance, licensing, external outreach, and Unicode-related action.

## Evidence standard

Claims must distinguish observed fact, interpretation, open question, and recommendation. Sources should be direct, durable, and attributable where possible. Vendor artwork is evidence, not source material to copy.

## Unicode boundary

The registry is independent from Unicode. Unicode-related work remains preparatory until the evidence supports a public plain-text communication need, stable repertoire, non-duplication, and an implementable character proposal.

- [Unicode character proposal FAQ](https://www.unicode.org/faq/char_proposal.html)
- [SEW guidelines](https://sew.unicode.org/guidelines)

## Versioning

Registry releases, schemas, artwork, and tooling use [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). See [docs/versioning.md](docs/versioning.md).
